# Figures

Key inventors, artists, and engineers in the history of analog video synthesis.

## Priority Figures (v1 Devices)

### Nam June Paik (1932-2006)
**Role:** Artist, Conceptual Pioneer
**Devices:** Paik/Abe Video Synthesizer (1969)
**Location:** New York, USA

Korean-American artist who pioneered video art. Collaborated with engineer Shuya Abe to create the first dedicated video synthesizer.

**Key contributions:**
- First video synthesizer
- Concept of "electronic superhighway"
- Video art as fine art medium

**Archive:** [Smithsonian American Art Museum](https://americanart.si.edu/artist/nam-june-paik-3670)

---

### Shuya Abe (1932-2021)
**Role:** Engineer, Circuit Designer
**Devices:** Paik/Abe Video Synthesizer (1969)
**Location:** Tokyo, Japan / New York, USA

Japanese engineer who designed the circuits for the Paik/Abe synthesizer. His technical expertise enabled Paik's artistic vision.

**Key contributions:**
- Amplitude window colorization circuits
- 7-channel matrix mixer design
- Wobbulator scan modulation
- Folding amplifier for nonlinear effects

**Archive:** [Experimental Television Center](http://experimentaltvcenter.org/shuya-abe)

---

### Dan Sandin (1942-)
**Role:** Engineer, Artist, Educator
**Devices:** Sandin Image Processor (1971-1974)
**Location:** Chicago, USA (University of Illinois)

Created the Distribution Religion - freely sharing schematics for video processing equipment.

**Key contributions:**
- Open-source hardware before open-source existed
- Amplitude classifier (quantized colorization)
- Four-quadrant multiplier for video
- Modular patch-based architecture

**Archive:** [SAIC](https://www.saic.edu/profiles/faculty/dan-sandin)

---

### Steve Rutt & Bill Etra
**Role:** Engineers, Inventors
**Devices:** Rutt/Etra Video Synthesizer (1972-1974)
**Location:** New York, USA

Created the iconic raster manipulation synthesizer known for its "terrain" effect.

**Key contributions:**
- Z-axis displacement (luminance controls geometry)
- DC-coupled video path
- Real-time 3D-like effects from 2D video

---

### Dave Jones
**Role:** Engineer
**Devices:** Jones Colorizer (1974)
**Location:** USA

Created simple, buildable colorizer designs that were widely replicated.

**Key contributions:**
- Multi-level keying with diode mixing
- Accessible DIY designs
- Layered color assignment

---

### Lars Larsen
**Role:** Engineer, Entrepreneur
**Devices:** LZX Industries (2008-present)
**Location:** Portland, USA

Modernized video synthesis with Eurorack-format modules and open-source designs.

**Key contributions:**
- Eurorack video synthesis standard
- Open-source Cadet series
- 1V video standard for modular systems

**Archive:** [LZX Industries GitHub](https://github.com/lzxindustries)

## Research Approach

For each figure:
1. **Biography** - Life, training, influences
2. **Devices** - What they built
3. **Techniques** - What methods they pioneered
4. **Documents** - Patents, papers, interviews
5. **Archives** - Where their work is preserved
