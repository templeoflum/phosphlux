# CA3054 - Dual Independent Differential Amplifier

## Role in Paik/Abe Synthesizer
Dual differential amplifier used in the folding amplifier section. Provides two independent diff amps on a single substrate for nonlinear signal processing.

## Key Specifications (from RCA databooks)
- Package: 14-pin DIP
- Contains: Two independent differential amplifiers + constant current transistors
- Six NPN transistors, fT > 300MHz
- Useful from DC to 120MHz
- Manufacturer: RCA -> Intersil -> Renesas (obsolete)
